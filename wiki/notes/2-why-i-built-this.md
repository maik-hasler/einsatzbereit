I built Einsatzbereit because I want to strengthen volunteering in the Oldenburg area, concretely. Not another large, anonymous platform, but something that helps local associations and organizations show where hands are needed right now, and that removes the first hurdle for people who actually want to help.

The second reason is personal: right now I barely manage to volunteer regularly myself - job, appointments, the usual. That bothers me. Einsatzbereit is my way of contributing anyway: not through my own time on site, but by building the infrastructure that makes it easier for others to do exactly what I currently don't have time for. My contribution to society runs through code here, not through volunteer hours.

Third, it started out as a showcase project too - something to point to. That has shifted: shipping it for the Oldenburg region is now the top priority, ahead of the showcase angle.

It also stays a sandbox project for me. A place to try out new technologies, architecture approaches, and ways of working, with a real goal behind it instead of a throwaway tutorial. Right now I'm especially drawn to trying things out in the LLM space, because the experience there carries over directly into my day job. That is exactly why clean code and a solid architecture matter to me here: I want to learn something myself, not just ship something.

The thread running through all of this: it only counts if it does something real for people in Oldenburg, and I come out of it better at what I do.
